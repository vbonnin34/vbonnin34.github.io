01

Mechanical prosthetic hand for a finger amputee

CAD3D scanningMechanism design

Designed a fully mechanical prosthetic hand for a patient who had lost all fingers in an accident, with no electronic components. Reconstructed a 3D scan of the patient's residual hand (STL format) and built a custom-fitted device around it — reworking complex organic surfaces and boolean operations in CAD to match the patient's anatomy.

The core challenge was enabling one-motion closure: elastic cords anchored at the fingertips are routed across the palm to the wrist, so that flexing the wrist inward tensions the cords and closes all fingers simultaneously.

  • Fully functional prototype achieving the target one-motion grip
  • Identified a ratchet-lock mechanism as the next improvement, to hold the hand closed for carrying heavier objects
  • Duration: 6 weeks
02

Pediatric tracheostomy training mannequin

3D SlicerMedical imagingCATIAOnshape

Built a realistic training mannequin for surgical residents to practice pediatric tracheostomy — a procedure with very few dedicated training tools available. Reconstructed the full anatomy (skeleton, trachea, organs) from DICOM medical imaging files, then cleaned up and optimized the resulting geometry across 3D Slicer, Meshmixer, CATIA, and Onshape for manufacturing.

Focused on biomechanical realism: designed an articulated neck joint using a rigid silicone tube fixed through the vertebrae to the skull, reproducing the cervical motion of tilting an infant's head back during the procedure — alongside precise internal organ placement and custom silicone molds.

  • Delivered a first functional version with cervical articulation, tracheostomy cannula, and torso support structure
  • Planned next steps: silicone neck skin and internal fill material for added tactile realism
  • Duration: 6 weeks
03

High-precision vibration generator for MMG sensor calibration

ArduinoPID controlMechatronics

Developed, during my internship at Inria Montpellier (CAMIN team), a complete mechatronic system generating tightly controlled vibration frequencies to calibrate 6-axis MMG (mechanomyogram) biomechanical sensors.

The system drives a DC motor through a cam-follower mechanism to vibrate a rod, closed-loop by an LM393 laser sensor to correct drift in real time and guarantee reproducible frequencies. Sized the cam profile and spring to produce a clean sinusoidal oscillation without overloading the motor torque, and tuned the PID control constants using the Ziegler–Nichols method.

  • Achieved stable, reproducible vibration frequencies for sensor calibration
  • Full mechanical design, prototyping, and closed-loop control implementation
